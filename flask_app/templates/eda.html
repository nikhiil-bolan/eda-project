Key Statistical Findings & Strategic Recommendations

1. Two-Wheeler Dominance (55.2%)

Urban EV adoption in India is led by 2W scooters and 3W commercial cargo rickshaws. Public infrastructure requires dedicated swappable battery bays and lightweight 3.3kW ports.

2. Critical Northern Corridors (UP & Bihar)

Uttar Pradesh and Bihar exhibit high adoption growth (48%+ YoY) driven by electric rickshaws, but possess severe charging deficits with over 450 EVs per public charger.

3. Balanced Southern Hubs (KA & MH)

Karnataka and Maharashtra maintain balanced ratios below 130:1, with robust private DISCOM tie-ups and high fast-charging penetration along key highway expressways.
